More about me :

I am a PhD student in computer science at the Université de Montréal (UdeM), in operations research, supervised by Margarida Carvalho and Utsav Sadana. My thesis is about robust and risk-averse integer programming games.
Before that I completed a master's degree in operations research at the UdeM (2024-2026), on the optimal placement of sensors to predict environmental data over a region, and a bachelor's degree in mathematics and computer science (2021-2024).
I am a French citizen with permanent resident status in Canada.
I love traveling, manga and computer science.
